About Bosh Civils

Infrastructure with a purpose beyond the project.

Bosh Civils (PTY) Ltd is a 100% black-owned and managed civil engineering and construction company, established in June 2020 with a purpose-driven mission — to transform communities through infrastructural excellence.

Guided by the principle that knowledge is power, we deliver industry-leading services in general maintenance, building construction, electrical works, and plant & machinery hire.

We are more than a service provider. We are partners in development, driven by impact, empowerment, and long-term sustainability.
